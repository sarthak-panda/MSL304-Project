MSL304 course project

How many security lanes should be open?

Too few lanes and the line explodes. Too many and you pay idle staff. FlowSim simulates every passenger and picks the lane count with the lowest total cost.

How to use
  1. Press Play the morning rusha 4 hour rush plays out
  2. Watch both hallssame passengers, today's 4 lanes vs FlowSim's plan
  3. Read the ringsgreen on time, amber close, red late
  4. Pause, scrub or go 8xcontrols sit above the halls
  5. Try Quiet midday or Holiday peaksometimes the plan closes lanes
